# Research Basis

DoLR documents ULPIN/Bhu-Aadhaar as a 14-digit land-parcel identity based on georeferenced coordinates and cadastral maps. NAKSHA is an urban land-record modernization programme using aerial/field survey and GIS workflows; its documented methodologies include oblique imagery and LiDAR and can produce 3D reality models. Therefore "drone + LiDAR + 3D map" is not sufficient novelty.

ISO 19152-2:2025 models land registration including spatial units, legal space of buildings/utility networks, geometry and topology. OGC CityGML 3.0 supports semantic 3D city objects, geometry, topology and external references.

Prototype wedge: preserve existing parcel identity + create explicit machine-readable 3D child units + provenance + deterministic validation + human review + traceable export.

This document records research context; it is not a claim of government endorsement.
